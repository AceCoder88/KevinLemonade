Space Station shop

Tap the sun or a planet to visit it.

More info

Every place in space has its own fruit and its own Kevin watching his family get squeezed.

Sun: pineapples. Moon: blueberries. Mars: strawberries. Jupiter: watermelons. Saturn: grapes. Turtlanda: turtles, watched by Shelby the turtle. Venus: cherries. Mercury: peppers. Neptune: coconuts. Pluto: kiwis.

Tap stuff on the space map too: asteroids (space gems), comets, Big Bertha the space whale, floating treasure chests, and the black hole (anything can come out!).

Kevin flies with you as your co-pilot. Do the space missions, shop at the Space Station (top left), and when you're ready... face Darth Lime at the Lime Star.

Each full pitcher sells for money that goes back home with you. Tap Earth to go home.
